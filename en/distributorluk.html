05

Frequently asked

Can the product be bought now?

No. NARION is at T0; the first 500-unit series ships at T4. Only distributor discussions and pilot-venue applications are open at this stage.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Because the first series is limited, volume tiers are set in conversation. We do not publish a fixed MOQ — on a 500-unit series that would not be honest.

Is the product certified?

We run the SABER/SASO (Saudi Arabia) and EAC (Russia / EAEU) applications in parallel with the pilot series. Food-contact material reports are at the same stage.

Is private label possible?

Not on the first series. The brand mark is laser-etched under the base only; private label will be considered after T4 against a volume commitment.

Why is there no price list on the site?

Dealer pricing varies by market, volume and channel. Publishing a fixed list would also undermine the distributor's own pricing.
