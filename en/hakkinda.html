Deep Heritage, Precise Engineering

Redefining a 500-Year-Old
Ritual.

NARION was founded to keep the grace and the shared ritual of a centuries-old hookah culture, while doing away with what the traditional industry has long overlooked: fragile glass, toxic carbon monoxide and the cumbersome business of managing coals.

The NARION Guarantee

NARION Hookah Systems is machined to micron precision at 5-axis CNC facilities that supply the defence and medical industries. All intellectual property and patents, from design through to software, belong to our company.

Our Philosophy

Three Commitments Behind Every NARION

🛡️

Built Not to Break

Traditional blown glass shatters at the slightest knock against a table, or under thermal shock. NARION is the first brand to bring impact-resistant Eastman Tritan™ TX1001 copolyester, used in aerospace, into a hookah body.

⚡

Zero Carbon Monoxide

The headache and the heavy-headed feeling do not come from the tobacco; they come from the 1,200+ ppm of toxic CO given off by burning charcoal. With an MCH ceramic-element Smart HMD we have brought that gas down to 0.0 ppm.

🔒

45° Bayonet

Rusted screws with stripped threads and loose silicone gaskets that pick up odour over time are a thing of the past. A medical-grade bayonet, locked with a single 45-degree turn of the wrist, holds its air seal for the life of the product.
