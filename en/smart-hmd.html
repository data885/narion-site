Real-World Pre-Heat Physics: Why 3.5 – 4.5 Minutes?

Exposed thin-wire coils glow in 60 seconds but scorch the top tobacco layer. Heating NARION's solid Ø57.8×5 mm Al₂O₃ ceramic plate, 4.5 mm 6061-T6 thermal base (21.8 kJ thermal capacitance), and 12–18 g of moist molasses tobacco evenly without combustion takes 3.5 – 4.5 minutes — 3× faster than waiting 12–15 minutes on a charcoal burner, with dense, unburnt clouds from puff one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key Engineering & Usage Answers

Does it use e-liquid or real hookah tobacco?
100% real hookah tobacco. Pack 12–18 g of your favourite Blonde Leaf, Dark Leaf, or herbal shisha into the Ø64 mm Flavour Pod or your existing phunnel bowl.
Why is pre-heat 3.5 – 4.5 minutes instead of 75 seconds?
Bringing the solid Al₂O₃ ceramic disk, 4.5 mm 6061-T6 base (21.8 kJ capacitance), and moist tobacco to 210°C evenly without scorching takes 3.5–4.5 minutes — 3× faster than a 15-minute coal stove, with zero tending.
Will it fit my existing traditional hookah stems?
Yes. The Ø67.5 mm base rim fits standard phunnel bowls directly, the included short/long medical silicone adapters fit all traditional stems, and the 45° bayonet locks onto NARION models.
Does the outer shell get hot or risk burns if knocked over?
Nano-Aerogel + PEEK insulation keeps the outer body below 42°C, SmCo magnets hold the head secure, and the 6-axis IMU cuts heater power in 200 ms if tilted past 35°.
