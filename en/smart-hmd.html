Internal Architecture & Craft

Four Layers of Precision Engineering

Every part of the Smart HMD is optimised to keep the outer shell cool and the tobacco vaporising evenly.

LAYER 1 · AEROSPACE SHELL

6061-T6 anodised aluminium body

A high-strength brushed outer shell that stays safe to handle at the table.

LAYER 2 · THERMAL BARRIER

Nanoporous aerogel insulation

Directs heat downwards into the tobacco only, keeping the electronics and the battery cool.

LAYER 3 · THE BRAIN

ESP32-C3 & PID thermal controller

Reads the temperature 50 times a second and balances the current to the airflow within milliseconds.

LAYER 4 · THE CORE

MCH medical-grade ceramic heater

The plate in direct contact with the tobacco is food-certified: no metallic taste, no released toxins.
