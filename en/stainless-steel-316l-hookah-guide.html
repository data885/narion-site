Frequently Asked Questions

Why use a 45° Bayonet lock instead of classic screw threads?

A quarter-turn Bayonet seal opens and locks effortlessly in one motion without cross-threading during rapid lounge washes.

Are glass bases and replacement stems interchangeable?

Yes. Modular stems, bases, and trays share uniform CNC tolerances on our Parts page.
