Frequently Asked Questions

Does Smart HMD fit standard hookahs?

Yes. Its dual base architecture works with both universal silicone-grommet sockets and NARION 45° Bayonet stems.

How long does a single battery cartridge last?

Each 74 Wh (14.8V Samsung 50S) cartridge runs for 90 to 110 minutes continuously (180–220 minutes across two cartridges).
