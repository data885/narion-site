Preparing for production

Design, engineering and quality control are run by NARION; production is carried out with certified manufacturing partners. The first run is in production preparation: drawings are done, CNC sampling is under way. The dimensions and performance figures on this site are our production decisions; verified test results will be published on the same pages as they come in.

Material decisions

Glass breaks. We ruled glass out

“Impact resistant” is not a marketing line but the result of a material choice. The three parts that break on a hookah are the vessel, the bowl and the hose tip. We ruled out all three; what replaced them, and what that earns a café, is below.

01

Shared architecture — identical on all three models

This is where the commercial logic of the family sits. These six details are identical across the range; differentiation is limited to size and finish.

01

Bayonet connection

No threads between body and bottle or body and bowl. A three-channel bayonet locking in a quarter turn. Fitted and removed one-handed, with no risk of stripping a thread.

02

Monoblock stem

Body and immersion stem are machined as a single part. With no weld and no threaded joint, the classic leak point is entirely absent.

03

Diffuser tip

Eight radial holes at the end of the stem. Smaller bubbles, lower draw noise and lower draw resistance.

04

Diaphragm purge

A silicone diaphragm instead of a ball valve, designed to run silently, resist clogging and come out for washing. In café use the ball valve is classically the first part to fail.

05

Measured draw resistance

Draw resistance is measured per model and printed on the technical sheet. Almost no competitor publishes this figure — in the upper segment it is the cheapest way to build technical trust.

06

Full strip-down

Seven parts without tools; all parts are intended to be dishwasher safe.

Why Tritan

The only sensible option that combines the clarity of glass with impact resistance. BPA-free, withstands 70 °C dishwasher cycles, arrives with food-contact certification. Its one known risk is flavour retention: series production will not begin before a blind odour panel is run after fifty wash cycles.

Coating decision

No paint and no chrome. In Gulf summer heat paint blisters and chrome peels. PVD (TiN / ZrN) does not scratch and does not fade under UV. This is a durability decision rather than an aesthetic one — coating thickness will be measured on every batch.
