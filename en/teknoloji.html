The weaknesses that have dogged the traditional hookah for centuries — broken glass bases, stripped threads, gaskets that hold odour and toxic charcoal fumes — have been removed by NARION's no-compromise engineering principles.

ENGINEERING MANIFESTO

A hookah should not merely look good. It should feel solid in the hand every time, hold its seal for a lifetime, and come out of the dishwasher as clear as it was on day one.

Crystal Clarity & Impact Strength

Eastman Tritan™ TX1001: Why Not Glass?

The table below sets out how Tritan, an aerospace copolyester, compares with traditional soda-lime glass and plexiglass.

Performance Criterion Traditional Blown Glass Acrylic / Plexiglass Eastman Tritan™ TX1001 (NARION)
Impact & drop resistance None (shatters on contact with the floor) Moderate (crazes over time) Outstanding (high impact strength)
Thermal shock resistance Low (fails on hot/cold transition) Moderate (deforms at 60°C) No deformation between −40°C and +100°C
Odour & aroma retention Good Poor (anise and mint odours linger) Excellent (food-certified, no retention)
Dishwasher compatibility Risk of clouding Never (melts or cracks) Fully compatible (as clear after hundreds of washes)
Chemical safety May carry a lead risk Contains BPA Entirely free of BPA, BPS and phthalates (FDA approved)
Mechanical Precision

45° Bayonet Lock

Forget screws that take an age to turn, seize up with limescale and strip their threads. NARION's patented three-channel bayonet locks the body to the base with a single turn of the wrist.

MEDICAL-GRADE SEALING

FKM fluoroelastomer O-ring

A medical gasket standard that absorbs no aroma, does not soften in hot water and keeps its elasticity for ten years.

MONOBLOC STEEL DOWNSTEM

Seamless AISI 304 Stainless Steel

The downstem is machined from a single piece of stainless steel; with no welds or threaded joints, an air leak is not possible.

ACOUSTIC SILENT DIFFUSER

Micro-perforated water silencer

It breaks large bubbles into micro-particles, bringing the gurgle down to a whisper and smoothing the draw.

Surface Engineering

PVD titanium nitride coating

Physical vapour deposition (PVD), as used on fine Swiss watches, bonds to the steel and aluminium body at a molecular level.

  • It never peels, flakes or tarnishes
  • Fully resistant to tobacco syrup and citrus acids
  • Available in brushed matte anthracite, champagne brass and titanium grey
