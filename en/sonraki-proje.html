Phase 2 Materials Engineering

Why Are We Engineering Eastman Tritan™?

Alongside our handcrafted Series 01 crystal glass bases, here is how our upcoming Phase 2 shatterproof polymer base solves commercial lounge breakage:

💎

Eastman Tritan™

Neither ordinary acrylic (PMMA) nor polycarbonate. A 0% BPA copolyester immune to stress-cracking from mint, anise, or citrus essential oils—delivering Bohemian crystal clarity (92% light transmission).

🔒

Insert-Molded 45° Bayonet

Instead of odor-trapping rubber grommets or chemical glues, the precision AISI 304 stainless steel collar is over-molded directly into molten polymer—locking hermetically in a single 45° quarter-turn.
