FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the NARION warranty?

Every model ships with a laser-engraved serial number and a certificate of authenticity; the body is covered by a 2-year warranty.

Which bowl comes with the models?

The standard bowl on every model is the NARION Smart HMD smart bowl. A classic ceramic bowl is available as an optional spare part.

Can I get spare parts?

Wear parts such as gaskets, the handle, the hose and the bowl can be replaced individually. KOZA, LADİN and MANOLYA share the same bayonet standard, so these parts are common to all three. See the current list on the Parts Shop page.

How does pre-registration work? Do I pay now?

No card details are requested at pre-registration. When Series 01 production is complete, our representative confirms with you on WhatsApp before shipping. Pre-registration form

How is the product delivered?

Every model is delivered in a magnetic presentation box with dedicated foam cradles.

Do you offer a solution for cafés and lounges?

Yes. The Business (B2B) page covers two commercial models, HaaS rental and outright purchase, plus on-site demo and quote requests.
