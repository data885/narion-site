Single-SKU logic

Because KOZA, LADİN and MANOLYA use the same bayonet standard, the bowl, the gaskets and the handle are the same part on all three. A café running all three models still stocks one spare-parts set, and ordering the wrong part stops being possible.

Where the maintenance burden goes

On a classic hookahOn a NARIONWhy
Glass base breakageRemoved from the designA Tritan base is impact-resistant; expect scratches rather than breakage.
Clay bowl crackingRemoved from the designEvery model ships with the NARION Smart HMD smart bowl as standard; no clay bowl and no charcoal. For those who want a classic bowl, the replacement bowl is cordierite technical ceramic.
Valve failureRemoved from the designThere is no ball valve; the diaphragm comes out and is washed.
Stripped threadsRemoved from the designThere are no threaded joints; the bayonet locks in a single 45° movement.
Gasket ageingPlanned spareAn FKM O-ring is a cheap consumable; annual replacement is recommended.

Why one bayonet?

One motion

Turn 45° and it locks. No threads, no stripping.

One stock

The same collar, seals and adapters on every model, so fewer wrong parts.

Replace only what broke

If a glass bottle breaks, the metal collar can be reused; only the glass is replaced.
