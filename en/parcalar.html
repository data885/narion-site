Why one bayonet?

One motion

Turn 45° and it locks. No threads, no stripping.

One stock

The same collar, seals and adapters on every model, so fewer wrong parts.

Replace only what broke

If a glass bottle breaks, the metal collar can be reused; only the glass is replaced.
