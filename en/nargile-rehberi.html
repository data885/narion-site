1. What Is an Electric Hookah Bowl (Smart HMD) and How Does Charcoal-Free Shisha Work?

An electric smart hookah bowl (Smart HMD) is not an e-cigarette or liquid vape. Patent-protected NARION Smart HMD works with 100% real molasses shisha tobacco—both blonde and dark leaf—replacing burning coconut coal with a food-grade Al₂O₃ ceramic heating core governed by a PT1000 closed-loop PID sensor.

It reaches target brewing temperature in roughly 3.5 minutes and holds your exact degree setting (from 150°C to 240°C) via the built-in 0.96-inch OLED dial or the BLE 5.0 NARION Smart mobile app without scorching the top tobacco layer.

FAQ

Is NARION Smart HMD compatible with my existing hookah pipe?
Yes. Its dual-fit base mounts seamlessly onto standard silicone-grommet phunnel setups as well as all NARION 45° bayonet stems.
How long does a single battery cartridge last?
One 74 Wh (14.8V Samsung 21700-50S) cartridge powers 90–110 minutes of continuous smoking, and swapping in a fresh charged cartridge takes 5 seconds.
Do I still need aluminum foil, tongs, or a coal burner?
No. Foil, coal burners, wind covers, and ash trays are completely eliminated—just pack your favourite shisha leaf and lock the upper thermal module.
Can I wash the tobacco chamber under running water?
Yes. The upper electronics and battery module detaches in a quarter turn so the lower tobacco chamber washes safely under warm water.
Where are NARION hookahs and Smart HMD units manufactured?
All NARION systems are engineered and CNC-machined in Tuzla, Istanbul, Türkiye (Aydıntepe Mah., Hudut Sok. No: 1/4) with worldwide B2B shipping.
